🍫 Strawberry Cake Recipe

Strawberry cake is a sweet cake flavored with strawberry puree or essence.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup strawberry puree or essence

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy.
  3. Add eggs one by one and beat well.
  4. Mix strawberry puree or essence.
  5. Add flour and baking powder alternately with milk.
  6. Pour into pan and bake for 30 minutes.
