🍫 Halwa Recipe

Halwa is a traditional sweet made by cooking flour with ghee and sugar.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup wheat flour / semolina
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup ghee
  • Water
  • Cardamom

Steps

  1. Roast flour in ghee.
  2. Add water and cook well.
  3. Add sugar and cardamom.
  4. Stir until thick.
