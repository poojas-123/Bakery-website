🍫 Whitebread Recipe

Order Now

White bread is soft bread made from refined flour and yeast.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Warm water
  • 2 tbsp oil

Steps

  1. Activate yeast in warm water with sugar.
  2. Add flour, salt, and oil.
  3. Knead into soft dough.
  4. Rest for 1 hour.
  5. Bake at 190°C for 30 minutes.
