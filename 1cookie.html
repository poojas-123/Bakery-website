🍫 Butter Cookie Recipe

Butter cookies are crisp cookies made with rich butter flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • ½ tsp vanilla essence

Steps

  1. Cream butter and sugar.
  2. Add flour and vanilla essence.
  3. Shape cookies.
  4. Bake at 170°C for 15 minutes.
