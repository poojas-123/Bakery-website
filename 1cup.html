🍫 Chocolate Cupcake Recipe

Chocolate cupcakes are small chocolate-flavored cakes baked in paper cups.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • Add egg, milk, and oil.
  • Fill cupcake liners halfway.
  • Bake for 18–20 minutes.

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix dry ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Add egg, milk, and oil.
  4. Fill cupcake liners halfway.
  5. Bake for 18–20 minutes.
