🍫 Honey Cookie Recipe

Honey cookies are soft cookies sweetened using natural honey.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ¼ cup honey
  • ½ tsp baking powder

Steps

  1. Mix butter and honey.
  2. Add flour and baking powder.
  3. Shape cookies.
  4. Bake at 170°C for 15 minutes.
