🍫 Redvelvet Cupcake Recipe

Red velvet cupcakes are soft red-colored cupcakes with mild cocoa flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • Red food color
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ tsp baking soda

Steps

  1. Cream butter and sugar.
  2. Add egg and red color.
  3. Mix cocoa powder and flour.
  4. Add milk and baking soda.
  5. Bake at 180°C for 20 minutes.
