🍫 Vanilla Cupcake Recipe

Vanilla cupcakes are soft mini cakes with a sweet vanilla flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup sugar
  • ½ cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

Steps

  1. Beat butter and sugar.
  2. Add egg and vanilla essence.
  3. Mix flour and baking powder.
  4. Add milk slowly.
  5. Bake for 18–20 minutes.
