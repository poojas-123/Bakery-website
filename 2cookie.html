🍫 Chocochip Cookie Recipe

Choco chip cookies are crunchy cookies filled with chocolate chips.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup chocolate chips
  • ½ tsp baking soda

Steps

  1. Mix butter and sugar.
  2. Add flour and baking soda.
  3. Mix chocolate chips.
  4. Bake at 180°C for 15 minutes.
