🍫 Gulabjammun Recipe

Gulab jamun is a soft milk-based sweet soaked in sugar syrup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk powder
  • ¼ cupflour
  • Baking soda
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Cardamom

Steps

  1. Make dough using milk powder and flour.
  2. Shape small balls.
  3. Fry until golden brown.
  4. Soak in warm sugar syrup.
