🍫 Rasgulla Recipe

Rasgulla is a spongy sweet made from chenna cooked in sugar syrup.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Lemon juice
  • Sugar
  • Water

Steps

  1. Boil milk and curdle with lemon juice.
  2. Strain and knead chenna.
  3. Shape balls.
  4. Boil in sugar syrup for 15 minutes.
