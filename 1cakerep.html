🍫 Chocolate Cake Recipe

Rich and moist chocolate cake perfect for any occasion. Soft texture with delicious chocolate flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • ½ cup hot water

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C and grease a cake pan.
  2. Mix flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and baking soda.
  3. In another bowl, beat eggs and sugar until creamy.
  4. Add oil, milk, and vanilla essence.
  5. Slowly mix dry ingredients with wet mixture.
  6. Pour batter into pan and bake for 30–35 minutes.
  7. Cool before serving.
