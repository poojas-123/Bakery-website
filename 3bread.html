🍫 Wheatbread Recipe

Wheat bread is healthy bread made from whole wheat flour.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups wheat flour
  • 1 tbsp yeast
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Water
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Mix yeast, sugar, and water.
  2. Add wheat flour and salt.
  3. Knead well and rest.
  4. Bake at 190°C for 30 minutes.
