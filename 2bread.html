🍫 Cheesegarlicbread Recipe

Cheese garlic bread is bread topped with garlic butter and melted cheese.

Ingredients

  • Bread loaf
  • Butter
  • Garlic paste
  • Cheese
  • Mixed herbs

Steps

  1. Mix butter and garlic.
  2. Spread on bread.
  3. Add cheese and herbs.
  4. Bake until cheese melts.
